Signal — the campus edition.

A broadcast/newsprint approach: the product is a daily edition with a lead story, numbered bulletins, a live strip and a desk. Poster type carries the voice; the officer view becomes a wire desk. Same four screens and the same data as Field Ledger, so the comparison is honest. Accessibility is built in: the ticker pauses and is duplicated in the schedule, and nothing relies on colour alone.

Signal — system and accessibility

The bet: a campus already reads notices, posters and papers, so a product that behaves like a well-edited daily edition feels familiar and civic rather than corporate. The risk is noise — poster type plus a moving wire can overwhelm — so the officer desk is deliberately quiet and tabular, and the wire is the only thing that moves.

Palette

Paper #F4F2EC
Ink #0E0F0C · 17.2:1
Signal #C63312 · white 5.4:1
Signal deep #8F2209 · 7.8:1
Muted #5C5E52 · 5.9:1

Vermilion marks the live/urgent layer only: the wire label, the lead kicker, the primary action. Never body text.

Type

AntonDisplay · headlines, numerals, club names

ArchivoBody · 16px / 1.55 · labels 13px semibold

IBM Plex MonoData · times, spots, table heads, wire

Layout

  • 12 columns, 1180px max, 26px page gutters.
  • Lead story 5/3 split; bulletins 3/2 with a ruled sidebar.
  • Rules do the dividing: 1px hairlines, 3px section rules.
  • No radius except the wire label and stamps (2px).
  • Tables invert their header row; the desk is the only dense screen.

Motion

  • One lead reveal: 10px rise, 500ms, stagger 45ms.
  • Wire crawls 42s, pauses on hover, focus and the explicit button.
  • Hover inverts a bulletin row instead of lifting it.
  • Reduced motion: wire becomes a wrapped static list, reveals off, no transitions.
  • No autoplay audio, nothing flashes, no scroll effects.

Accessibility in this board

  • Skip link, landmarks, one h1 per screen, ordered headings.
  • All controls are buttons/links; the wire has a real pause control with aria-pressed.
  • Wire content is repeated in "On campus today", so nothing is only-in-motion.
  • Status is written: "waitlist open", "38 / 60", "seats left" — not colour alone.
  • Focus ring 3px vermilion with 3px offset; 44px primary targets.
  • Body 17.2:1, muted 5.9:1, white-on-signal 5.4:1; tables use th scope and captions.
  • Reduced-motion path removes the crawl and all reveals; content stays visible without JS.

Honest risks

  • Poster type at 82px can push the lead below the fold on a phone — the clamp caps it.
  • Newsprint metaphors age; the desk must stay a tool, not a pastiche.
  • Two type families plus mono is the ceiling; adding a fourth breaks the system.
  • Vermilion is assertive — the desk deliberately uses almost none of it.
