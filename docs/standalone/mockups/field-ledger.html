System notes

The rules the screens are built from. Field Ledger sets the tone; Campus Press sets the discipline for data. Everything below is a token, not a one-off value.

Palette

Paper · #FAF7F0
Ink · #16170F
Leaf · #204C3A
Clay · #C2542F · pressure
Sun · #E9B44C · highlight
Rule · #DFD8C8

Type

Fraunces — statements only, 400/600, italic for emphasis.
Instrument Sans — body, 15px/1.55.
JetBrains Mono — times, counts, labels, 8.5–11px, uppercase labels.

Motion

Enter 480–520ms, 12px rise, 45ms stagger. Hover 180ms. Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). No parallax, no loops, nothing moves twice. Reduced motion removes all of it.

Non-negotiables

  • No shadows. 1px rules and paper tone separate surfaces.
  • One accent per screen: leaf. Clay means time pressure.
  • Serif never carries body copy.
  • Data views: zero radius, mono heads, tabular numbers.
  • 44px minimum tap target on phones.
